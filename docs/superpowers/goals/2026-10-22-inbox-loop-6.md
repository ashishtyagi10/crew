# Loop — the inbox, a sixth five rounds

**Status: IN PROGRESS.** Set by the user after the fifth loop closed
(`2026-10-22-inbox-loop-5.md`): *"run the inbox enhancement loop 5 more times"*.

## Rules that hold every round

As in the loops before, which the user set:

- No feature the user did not ask for. A round fixes, simplifies or polishes what is there.
- `r` replies to everyone on the mail; there is no other reply, and no Bcc.
- The list, the open mail and the reply box are each a card, and the mail's card is always
  taller than the reply box.
- Every surface is judged on both pages; every file stays under the 200-line cap; the foot
  keeps every key visible; nothing is fetched that was not fetched before; no scratch file
  stays in the tree while `scripts/release.sh` runs.
- Nothing a test does opens a browser, reads the real Downloads or the real grant.

## One round

1. **Survey** by walking a flow through its code, or by driving the pane at random
   (`boxfuzz_tests`).
2. **Pick** what a reader would notice most, none of it a new feature.
3. **Build** it with tests that fail without it, release, and log the round below.

## Log

**Round 1 (v0.27.78): no panic at any size, and tests that open nothing.**

- **The survey** (a new one: the pane driven at random, keys, clicks, wheels, pastes and
  Gmail's answers interleaved, at sizes from one cell to a wide screen). Within seconds it
  found a panic: a forward's box on a pane a row or two tall drew its carried-mail line at
  `h - 2`, which underflowed (a debug panic; in a release, a line drawn far off the box).
- **The forward line is drawn only in a box tall enough for it** (`composedraw.rs`).
- **The random drive is a test now** (`boxfuzz_tests.rs`, 150 seeded runs, about three
  seconds).
- **A cost of the survey, and its fix.** Its first long run reached the setup page (an ended
  sign-in) and pressed Enter there, and three ways of opening a browser were not the
  test-safe opener: the sign-in (`signin.rs`), the setup page's 1–5 and `o` (`keys.rs`); and
  the setup page read the real Downloads, where the user's client files were, under tests.
  So that run may have opened Google sign-in pages in the user's browser. All three now go
  through `linkclick::open_external`, which tests record and never open, and `setup::look`
  reads nothing under tests, as `grant::path` already did.

**Round 2 (v0.27.79): mail from anyone, mangled at random.**

- **The survey.** The two things the pane reads that anyone can write, driven at random: the
  corpus of real mail cut, spliced and laced with hostile HTML (huge spans, bad entities,
  unclosed comments, absurd CSS), read and laid out at every width, ten thousand of them in
  a debug build; and twenty thousand headers made of the pieces that break parsers
  (addresses, List-Unsubscribe, dates, subjects), through the parsers and the box's line
  editing. Neither panicked: nothing to mend in them. One thing seen on the way: a `to` or
  `cc` line being written measured itself again for every letter it dropped from the left
  (`window`), slower to draw the longer the line.
- **Both drives are tests now**, small enough for every release (`htmlfuzz_tests.rs`,
  `hdrfuzz_tests.rs`), so a change that would panic on a strange mail is caught.
- **A line being written is laid out in one pass** back from its caret (`composehead.rs`).
- **Tests:** two hundred addresses on the `cc` line show `…`, the last of them and the caret.

**Round 3 (v0.27.80): the mail window answers the mouse as the pane does.**

- **The survey.** Speed first: a thread of twenty real newsletters, timed in a debug build,
  redraws in about 0.6 ms, opens in 11 ms, and takes a key in 0.03 ms, so nothing to mend.
  Then where the pane meets the rest of crew: the mail window (`w`), drawn as the pane is,
  took the pane's keys but only Cmd/Ctrl+click of the mouse, so a plain click on a link, a
  keycap, the counter, a folded mail or the reply box did nothing there; and its wheel
  rounded each delta alone, so a slow trackpad (a few pixels a delta) never moved the mail,
  which the grid's wheel had fixed long before by carrying the fraction over.
- **A plain click in the window is the pane's click** (`inboxwin.rs`): it opens the link,
  does the keycap, steps the counter, opens the folded mail, moves the box's caret; Esc's
  keycap closes the window.
- **The window's wheel carries its fractions** (`mailwin::wheel_lines`, 24 pixels a line as
  the grid's).
- **Tests:** ten five-pixel deltas make two lines.

**Round 4 (v0.27.81): the hand in the mail window.**

- **The survey.** With clicks working in the mail window (round 3), it still never changed
  the pointer: no hand over a link, a keycap or the counter, and no wash on a list row under
  the pointer when the window was wide enough to show the list, as the tile has.
- **One test of what clicks** (`InboxPane::clickable_at`, `mouse.rs`), read by the tile's
  pointer (`inboxclick.rs`) and by the window's, which now turns to a hand there and washes
  the row under it (`inboxwin.rs`).
- **Tests:** the hand over a thread, a link, the counter and a keycap; none on a card's edge
  or on words that are not a link.
