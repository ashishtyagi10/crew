### Round 1 — the rain wears the palette's hue
Behaviour: the welcome's rain and a busy smith pane's code line fall in the
accent's hue, at `text_muted`'s lightness. Seen: violet rain on nebula, orange
on gruvbox, indigo on porcelain, blue on neon. Measured: rain/muted contrast
within 25% on all 20, hue within 12° of the accent on the 16 colour palettes.
Rows: none added, none removed. Shot: `theme_welcome_shot`.

### Round 2 — soft themes: boxes inside a card are wells
Behaviour: on porcelain, sand, mist and slate a round-cornered box crew draws
inside a pane (a settings field, the chat and to-do composers, far's panels)
loses its stroke and is pressed into the card at the input bar's resting
depth (-0.65). A box that holds boxes (a settings group) loses its rule and
keeps its legend. Terminals are left alone: a program's box is its output.
Seen: `theme_wells_shot`, `relief_window_shot`, `glass_work_shot`. Rows: none.
Idea: neumorphism.io's inset fields; the open item of 2026-11-12.

### Round 3 — soft themes: a code block is a well
Behaviour: on the soft themes a fenced code block in a chat (two or more rows
tinted from one column) loses its tint and is pressed into the card like the
other wells; an inline `code` chip stays a chip. Seen: `glass_work_shot` on
porcelain, sand, mist, slate. Rows: none. Other themes: byte-identical cells.

### Round 4 — slate: cards stand out of a dark page
Measured in `glass_work_shot`, a pixel row across the gutter: on porcelain,
sand and mist the dark shadow reads 1.75:1 against the card; on slate the
dark one read 1.08:1 and the light one 1.04:1, so its cards and wells were
close to invisible. A near-black page leaves the dark shadow nothing to
darken, so the light one is run further toward white (0.14 → 0.28): 1.22:1
at the edge, still the quietest thing on the page. Pale pages unchanged.

### Round 5 — soft themes: a well a panel
Behaviour: a box divided by a rule (`┬` on its top edge over `┴` on its
bottom: `/far`'s two file panels) is pressed in as one well a panel and the
rule is blanked; the second panel starts on the rule's column so its rim
clears the panel's first glyphs. Seen: `relief_window_shot`. Rows: none.
