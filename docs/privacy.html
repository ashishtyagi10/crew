Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

The short version

Crew is a desktop app that runs on your own computer. There is no Crew account and no Crew server. Crew collects no analytics, telemetry or crash reports, and the people who make Crew never receive your data. Crew talks only to the services you turn on, and it talks to them directly from your machine.

What stays on your computer

Your settings, sign-ins and usage history live in Crew's configuration folder on your computer. Agent smith keeps its conversation history and memory for each project in a .crew folder inside that project. Files that hold credentials, such as credentials.json and gmail.json, can be read only by your user account (permission 0600). Deleting these folders removes what Crew has stored.

Services Crew connects to

Crew sends data to another service only when you use a feature that needs it. Each service handles that data under its own privacy policy.

  • AI model providers. When you chat with agent smith or run a swarm, your prompt and the context it needs (for example, files or command output you share with it) go to the provider you chose, such as Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, NVIDIA, DeepSeek or Alibaba DashScope, or to a command-line agent you have installed, such as Claude Code.
  • Gmail, if you sign in through /inbox (see below).
  • Open-Meteo, for the weather card: a place name or coordinates, never your identity.
  • GitHub, to check for and download updates.
  • DuckDuckGo, when an agent searches the web for you.
  • Telegram, only if you connect a Telegram bot.

Gmail and Google user data

The /inbox pane shows your Gmail inside Crew. You sign in with a Google OAuth client that you create in your own Google Cloud project, and Crew asks for one scope, gmail.modify.

What Crew reads

Thread lists, message headers, message bodies and labels, which it fetches from the Gmail API so you can read and search your mail in the pane. It also reads your email address, to show which account is signed in.

What Crew changes, and only when you press a key

  • Archiving, starring, and marking as read or unread, which change labels on a thread.
  • Deleting, which moves a thread to Gmail's Trash, as Gmail's own delete does. Gmail empties Trash after 30 days; Crew never deletes mail permanently.
  • Opening a thread marks it read, as Gmail does.
  • Saving a reply as a Gmail draft. Crew never sends mail.

Where it is stored

The inbox pane keeps only the sign-in grant (your client ID, client secret and refresh token) in gmail.json on your computer, readable only by your user account. Access tokens stay in memory and expire within an hour. The pane does not save your mail to disk. A thread you hand to agent smith (below) becomes part of smith's conversation, which is saved with the rest of that conversation in the project's .crew folder. A command-line agent such as Claude Code also keeps its own history.

Sharing with an AI provider

When you press a (ask smith), r (draft a reply) or t (make a todo), the open thread is sent to the AI provider you configured, so that it can answer, write the draft, or find the deadline. Nothing is sent until you press one of those keys. The thread is passed to the model marked as untrusted content, with an instruction not to follow anything it says.

What Crew never does with Google data

Crew does not send Google user data to its developers or to any server they run. It does not sell the data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models, and it does not let anyone read it except you and the AI provider you choose for the actions above.

Crew's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Run /inbox signout to delete gmail.json and revoke the grant at Google. You can also remove access at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page.

Children

Crew is a developer tool and is not aimed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. The history of every change is public in the repository.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised by opening an issue on GitHub.
